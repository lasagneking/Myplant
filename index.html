These never appear apart in your data, so the pattern-matching above can't tell which one (if either) is actually responsible.

How this works

Ingredients are compared with symptom days in your local diary. Ingredients logged on almost every day, or without enough repeats, are left out — only ones noticeably above your own baseline symptom rate are shown. The more consistently you log, the more useful this becomes.
